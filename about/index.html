Benny Labs Foundation

About the Foundation

Benny Labs Foundation Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit public charity founded and led by Benny Goldstein. Its mission is to advance the public good through research, invention and education across technology and the creative fields.

Who our work is for

Our public digital resources are intended for people exploring technology, creators, students, developers and independent inventors. Our research explanations also welcome readers and specialists interested in how a proposed invention works.

The current public activities include AI cost education through Penny Owl, free desktop reading software through Voco, and openly readable invention explanations. Visit Programs and public resources for access instructions and development status.

Research, education and public benefit

The Foundation explores ideas that could make technology more useful and accessible, and shares public explanations so people can learn from that work. Research-stage projects are identified as concepts or projects in development.

A patent application or concept illustration is not proof of a finished product, validated performance or clinical benefit. Workshops and awards are educational aims; dates and participation terms will be published when an actual program is announced.

Our research-support model preserves inventor ownership and allows project-specific returns to support the Foundation’s charitable mission.

Our founder

Benny Goldstein founded and leads Benny Labs Foundation. His work connects invention, research and public learning. Technical specialists, researchers and educators can contact the Foundation to discuss a specific project or educational collaboration.
