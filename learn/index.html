Use these resources your way

Students can practice the worked examples. Creators and developers can use the calculator to check a budget. Independent inventors can use the evaluation steps to plan and document a small test. The guides are self-directed learning material; no certification or personalized advice is offered.

The calculator uses the numbers you enter on your device and does not send them to the Foundation. You do not need to provide payment details, sign in to an AI provider or leave this site to use it.

Explore a concept in detail
