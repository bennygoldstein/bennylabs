Research in development

ThermalCore

A research concept for a sealed computing core designed to recover heat for water heating. The project explores combining useful computation with heat recovery; measured prototype performance remains part of development.

Visit thermalcore.energy →
Research in development

Newhab

An investigational wearable exercise device intended to provide gentle, adjustable resistance for rehabilitation. In development, not available for sale and not FDA-cleared; treatment benefits have not been established.

Visit newhab.life →
Free public reading tool

Voco

A free desktop reading tool for Mac and Windows. Select text and listen instead of reading it on screen. Default on-device voices keep the selected text local; optional cloud voices send that reading to the speech provider.

Visit voco.talk →
Free community initiative

Penny Owl

A free AI savings guide from Benny Labs Foundation. Compare AI brain, image and video models, see combined leaderboard rankings, and find the lowest available API prices across multiple aggregators. We help creators, developers and curious people save money and spend less time searching.

Visit pennyowl.org →
Patent-pending invention

Transactional Traffic

A coordination and marketplace layer that assigns safe lane-level micro-maneuvers to nearby vehicles, verifies each completed action, and releases individualized rewards only after successful performance.

Explore the concept →
Patent-pending invention

Worm Cargo

A mobile cargo-routing network that connects vehicles into a temporary chain, sorts destination-addressed packages across the connected vehicles, and lets each vehicle continue with the right cargo already aboard.

Explore the concept →
Patent-pending invention

Living Nails

A wearable display system that keeps the nails thin by moving the battery, controller, and wireless hardware into elegant jewelry—then coordinates several nail screens as one expressive canvas.

Explore the concept →
Memory service in development

Lion Brain

A private memory service being developed to help people retain context across supported AI tools. Its public website explains access and limits. Connected assistants must actually read and save authorized information; complete capture is not automatic.

Our mission

We exist to help ideas become real — inventing, developing, and proving new technologies, and sharing knowledge and opportunity so that progress benefits society as a whole.

Benny Labs Foundation conducts, supports, and funds innovation wherever it can move humanity forward — advancing concepts from an early spark all the way to a validated proof of concept, and promoting education and creativity for both adults and youth.

What we do

An engine for invention — and for the public good

We combine hands‑on R&D with education, so breakthroughs get built and the next generation learns to build them.

Invent & develop

We invent new ideas, technologies, and systems — and advance them from an early‑stage concept to a validated, working proof of concept.

Patent & protect

We document invention concepts and pursue intellectual-property protection while sharing public explanations of the ideas and their intended uses.

License & scale

We seek technical and research collaboration to investigate how proposed technologies could reach people. A concept or patent application is not a completed product or a guarantee of performance.

Educate & inspire

We share public project explanations and digital tools for self-directed learning. Workshops, grants and prizes are future educational aims; this website does not currently announce dates or an open application round.

How we fund innovation

Supporting research and public learning

Our mission includes supporting early research, prototype development and public learning. Project-specific arrangements determine how work is supported and how it can benefit the community.

Inventors keep ownership

Our stated research-support model lets inventors retain ownership of their inventions. Funding and collaboration terms are project-specific; no public research-award application round is currently announced here.

We de‑risk the first step

The aim is to help investigate an idea, build and test a prototype, and share what is learned. Research concepts remain in development until their proposed performance has been demonstrated.

Returns recycle into the mission

Our research-support model allows a project-specific, capped share of successful sales to return to the Foundation. Any such Foundation proceeds support its charitable research and education mission.
