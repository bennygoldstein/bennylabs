New Foundation program · women's health

WePMS: free, honest help for PMS

Every month, millions of women deal with cramps, mood swings and bloating. WePMS gives them clear information so they can make good choices for their own bodies.

Foundation program · always free

WePMS.org

WePMS is a free resource from the Benny Labs Foundation that helps women understand and get through PMS. We gather the best information we can find from public sources, such as medical groups, health agencies and published research, and explain it in plain, kind words. There are no ads, no sales and no profit.

  • The basics of PMS, PMDD and the menstrual cycle, explained with pictures
  • Honest product guides that rate how much science backs each option
  • Wellness tips, period-tracking app comparisons and answers to common questions
  • A free weekly newsletter with the newest products and research

WePMS shares public information, not medical advice, and is not currently affiliated with any doctors. We hope OB-GYNs will join us.

Our mission

We exist to help ideas become real — exploring new technologies and sharing useful tools and knowledge so that progress can benefit society as a whole.

Our current public resources include original learning guides, an AI cost calculator and detailed invention explanations. Our research portfolio includes concepts in development, with proposed uses and testing needs identified separately from resources available today. Visit our Programs page to see who each resource serves and how to participate.

What we do

An engine for invention — and for the public good

We combine invention research with public tools and project explanations that help people explore technology and understand design choices.

Invent & develop

We develop invention concepts and describe their intended uses, design choices and testing needs. Proposed performance remains a research question until it has been demonstrated.

Patent & protect

We document invention concepts and pursue intellectual-property protection while sharing public explanations of the ideas and their intended uses.

License & scale

We seek technical and research collaboration to investigate how proposed technologies could reach people. A concept or patent application is not a completed product or a guarantee of performance.

Educate & inspire

We share public project explanations and digital tools for self-directed learning. Workshops, grants and prizes are future educational aims; this website does not currently announce dates or an open application round.

Learn without a purchase

The guides and calculator on BennyLabs.org are free to use. This website has no checkout or donation form, and no paid account is required to read the materials.
