My Prompts

Free tools you can get with a prompt. Pick one, copy its prompt, and paste it into Claude Code.

Taskbar Sliders

Volume, brightness and blue-light sliders that sit right on the Windows 11 taskbar. Hold the mouse on a knob to see its percentage. Paste a few lines into Claude Code and it installs them in about a minute.

Get Taskbar Sliders

Read Aloud Bar

Highlight text in any app, press play on the taskbar, and your computer reads it out loud with the voices already in Windows. No API key, no account, no internet and no .exe.

Get Read Aloud Bar
